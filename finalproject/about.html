About EduScope

EduScope started from a simple frustration: EdTech research is scattered across dozens of journals, institutions, and conference sites, with no single place to see them together. This directory brings the major global resources into one filterable, "bookmarkable" list, built for researchers, educators, and students working across regions and access levels.

This is an individual course project for WDD 231 at BYU-Idaho, built from scratch with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript
