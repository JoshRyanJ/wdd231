Find EdTech Research, Without the unnecessary Search Fatigue

EduScope brings together the journals, databases, organizations, and conferences that educational technology researchers rely on worldwide. Searchable, ,"bookmarkable, and in one place.

Explore the Directory

How EduScope Works

1. Browse

Explore a curated set of global EdTech research resources, each with region, access type, and category at a glance.

2. Filter

Narrow the list down by category: journals, databases, organizations, or conferences to find exactly what fits your research need.

3. Bookmark

Save resources you want to return to. Your bookmarks are stored right in your browser, so they're there next time you visit.

Why This Exists

Educational technology research is scattered across dozens of journals, institutions, and conference sites. Most of which aren't indexed together anywhere. EduScope exists to shorten that search, especially for researchers working across regions and institutions with different levels of access.
